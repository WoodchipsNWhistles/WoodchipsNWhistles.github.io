A Fibonacci poem regarding yet another war in my lifetime

Dave Mitchner

Written February 28, 2026 • Originally published: davemitchner.com + Facebook


War.
Why?
Again
Smedley said -
"It is a racket!"
The rich must profit
But avoid
Self risk
For
Cash.
